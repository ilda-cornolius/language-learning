What Lumina collects

Lumina is a language-learning app. Depending on how you use it, it may process:

  • Your Google account name, email, and ID token if you sign in with Google
  • Learning progress, saved words, flashcards, and preferences stored on your device
  • Words or messages you choose to sync online (Firebase Authentication and Firestore)
  • Microphone audio when you use pronunciation or voice practice (processed on-device or by the system speech service; we do not keep a recording library)
  • Camera images when you scan text for flashcards (processed on-device with ML Kit)
  • Prompts you send to the AI tutor (handled by Google Firebase AI)
  • Basic app analytics from Firebase Analytics

How it is used

We use this information to run your account, save your study data, provide tutoring and practice features, and understand whether the app is working. We do not sell your personal information.

Third parties

Sign-in, cloud save, analytics, and the AI tutor use Google / Firebase services. Their handling of data is described in Google’s privacy policy.

Your choices

You can use much of Lumina without signing in. You can sign out, skip microphone or camera permission, and clear app data from Android settings. To delete study data and keep your account, use Delete your Lumina data. To close the account as well, use Delete your Lumina account.
