Delete your Lumina data

This page is for the Android app Lumina (package com.lingualearn.pro). Use it to request deletion of some or all of your Lumina data without deleting your Lumina account. You can keep signing in.

How to request data deletion

  1. Choose what to delete in the form below (saved words, study progress, or all Lumina cloud study data). Include the Google email you use in Lumina.
  2. Tap Request data deletion, then submit the GitHub draft that opens so the Lumina team receives it. A free GitHub account is required to send it.
  3. We remove the requested cloud data within 30 days. Your Lumina account stays active unless you separately use the delete account page.
  4. To remove the same kinds of data stored only on your device, open Lumina and delete those items there, or Android Settings → Apps → Lumina → Storage → Clear data.

Request form

This does not close your Lumina account. To close the account as well, use Delete your Lumina account.

Data that can be deleted

  • Words, meanings, and example sentences saved to your online Lumina deck
  • Synced learning progress (XP, level, completed lessons and challenges, awards)
  • Other study fields stored in your Lumina cloud profile besides sign-in

Data that is kept, and for how long

  • Your Lumina account stays. We keep the Firebase sign-in needed to open the app with Google, including the display name and email on that account record.
  • Your Google Account is not changed.
  • Data we were not asked to delete stays (for example, if you only request word-list deletion, progress remains).
  • Data on your phone or tablet is not erased from here. Clear Lumina storage or delete items in the app to remove local flashcards, progress, and preferences.
  • Deletion of the requested cloud data is completed within 30 days. Encrypted backups may remain for up to 90 days, then expire and are not restored into the live app.
  • Firebase Analytics may keep aggregated or event data that does not identify you, according to Google’s Analytics retention (commonly up to 14 months).
  • The request itself may be kept up to 90 days as a record that we fulfilled it, then discarded or stripped of contact details.
