Delete your Lumina account

This page is for the Android app Lumina (package com.lingualearn.pro). Use it to request deletion of your Lumina account and the data stored with it.

Feedback Privacy policy Delete account

How to request deletion

  1. Fill in the form below with the Google email you use to sign in to Lumina, then tap Request deletion.
  2. Submit the draft that opens on GitHub so the Lumina team receives the request. A free GitHub account is required to send it.
  3. We delete your Lumina account and associated cloud data within 30 days of a complete request.
  4. On your device, uninstall Lumina or Android Settings → Apps → Lumina → Storage → Clear data to remove local study data we cannot erase remotely.

Request form

Requests without the email you signed in with may be delayed until we can match your account.

Data that is deleted

  • Your Lumina Firebase Authentication account (the app sign-in tied to Google)
  • Cloud profile fields stored for Lumina (display name, email on the Lumina user record)
  • Synced learning progress (XP, level, completed lessons and challenges, awards)
  • Words and example sentences saved to your online Lumina deck

Data that is kept, and for how long

  • Your Google Account is not deleted. Only the Lumina app account and Lumina cloud data are removed.
  • Data on your phone or tablet (flashcards, local progress, preferences) stays until you clear Lumina’s storage or uninstall the app.
  • Deletion is completed within 30 days. Encrypted backups of cloud data may remain in routine backups for up to 90 days, then expire and are not restored into the live app.
  • Firebase Analytics may keep aggregated or event data that does not identify you, according to Google’s Analytics retention settings (commonly up to 14 months).
  • The deletion request itself (email and request text) may be kept up to 90 days as a record that we fulfilled it, then discarded or stripped of contact details.
  • Short AI tutor or speech snippets are not stored by Lumina as a user library. Provider logs, if any, follow Google’s Firebase / AI retention.
